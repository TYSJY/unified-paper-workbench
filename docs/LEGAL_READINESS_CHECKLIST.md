# Legal readiness checklist

This is a product-engineering checklist and not legal advice.

- Identify the seller, contracting entity, jurisdiction, tax/VAT obligations,
  invoicing method, and customer-support address.
- Define whether users bring their own model account/API key and who bears
  model usage costs.
- Disclose that generated research content, citations, data, authorship,
  declarations, journal choice, and final submission remain the user's
  responsibility.
- Review journal, publisher, ORCID, browser automation, e-mail, and model
  provider terms for automated access and account registration.
- Define explicit consent and audit semantics for final submission, withdrawal,
  retargeting, reviewer suggestions, declarations, and payments.
- Prohibit fabricated data, authors, reviews, credentials, and deceptive use.
- Define data roles, retention, deletion, subprocessors, cross-border transfer,
  breach notification, and data-subject rights.
- Finalise proprietary source licence, end-user licence, subscription terms,
  privacy policy, acceptable-use policy, and refund/cancellation policy.
- Complete third-party licence compliance, especially Qt/PySide distribution.
- Obtain trademark/domain review and avoid implying endorsement by OpenAI,
  Codex, ChatGPT, ORCID, publishers, journals, or universities.
